What we do

The bagging work you don't want on your floor

High-count, repetitive, easy to get wrong — and the first thing your customer notices when a bolt is missing. That's exactly the work we specialize in.

Hardware pack kitting

Complete assembly packs for furniture, playsets, grills, fitness equipment, mounts and more — built to your bill of materials, piece by piece.

Polybagging & sealing

Clear poly bags in the sizes your job needs, heat-sealed and presentation-clean. Bulk bags for distributors or single packs for retail boxes.

About Backyard Bagging

A family business that grew up around fasteners

Backyard Bagging started the way a lot of good trade businesses do — in the family. Owner James Joyner's father spent 46 years in the fastener business and built the kitting operation — and James took it over when he retired. That history matters: we don't just pack parts, we know what the parts are, what they're for, and what happens when the wrong one lands in a bag.

Today the company is headquartered in Louisville, Kentucky, with a hands-on operation in Houston, Texas — close to the port, major distribution centers, and customers across the South and Midwest. It's a lean model on purpose: no bloated warehouse overhead, just focused kitting work done carefully and priced fairly.

Ask about your job

Common questions

Do you supply the hardware, or do we? +

Either way. Many customers ship us their bulk hardware and we kit it. If you'd rather we help source standard fasteners, say so in your quote request — fasteners are our home turf.

Is there a minimum order? +

There's no published minimum — replacement-parts kits can be a few hundred packs, production runs can be many thousands. Send the quantity you have and we'll tell you honestly if it's a fit.

How fast can you turn a job around? +

It depends on count, pack complexity and volume. Rework and rescue jobs are handled as urgently as we can staff them — tell us your ship date up front and we'll quote to it or tell you straight if we can't hit it.

Can you match our labels and barcodes? +

Yes — send your label spec or a sample and we'll match part numbers, barcodes and layout on laser labels. If you don't have a spec yet, we'll help you set a clean one.

Where do finished packs ship? +

Back to you, to your distribution center, or wherever the job needs them. Our Houston operation is well placed for Texas and Gulf Coast distribution; Louisville covers the Midwest and East.
